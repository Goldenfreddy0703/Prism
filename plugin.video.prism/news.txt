Prism 5.0.58 — Stability and background polish

This update refines 5.0.57 with a quieter service, less background plugin traffic, and safer binge playback dialogs.

What's new in 5.0.58 (highlights)
- Lighter service loop and fewer background RunPlugin jobs while you browse or watch
- Library and browse lists verify or warm metadata when you open them, not in idle storms
- Widget refreshes coalesce better on Android and after library sync
- Fixes duplicate Playing Next / Skip Outro prompts after pause or playlist changes
- Safer handling when the next queue item is not a Prism source URL

Upgrading from 5.0.57
No data wipe required — update in place, restart Kodi once, and continue watching.

Upgrading from below 5.0.57
You still need a full wipe and Simkl sign-in as described in the 5.0.57 notes (see Changelog 5.0.57).

Report issues on GitHub or Addons4Kodi Discord.
