Prism 5.0.70 — Playlists and season packs

Rescrape and Source Select now start the episode in the playlist, so the following episodes are queued. Next Up stays closed on the last episode.

When a season pack plays, the next episode uses that same torrent and picks the file by the episode number in the name. A single-episode file, Rescrape, or Source Select clears the saved pack.
